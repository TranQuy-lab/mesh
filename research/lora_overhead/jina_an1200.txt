## How to Find Product Documentation

In an effort to ensure our customers always have the most updated product documentation, Semtech has improved the way documents are accessed on our website. The link which brought you to this page is no longer valid.

Documents are still available on our website. Here are few ways to find product documentation:

*   [Search by keyword, such as part number or brand name](https://www.semtech.com/search)
*   [Filter all documents by product platform, product family and document category](https://www.semtech.com/design-support/product-guides)
*   [Browse by Semtech product platform](https://www.semtech.com/products)
*   [Register for mySemtech to request access to private and confidential documents](https://semtech.force.com/DP?__hstc=212684107.fb352f55728c903d4f39365f59ffa934.1558113816185.1571847660315.1571859514517.529&__hssc=212684107.13.1571859514517&__hsfp=1385406553)
